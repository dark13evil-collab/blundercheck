# BlunderCheck

Free chess game review for your Lichess and Chess.com games.

- Pulls your recent games from the public Lichess and Chess.com APIs.
- Runs Stockfish 19 (lite) in your browser and classifies every move, including brilliant moves.
- Explains mistakes in plain English: hanging pieces, forks, pins, missed mates and more.
- Shows patterns across your games and turns your mistakes into practice puzzles.

Open it at https://dark13evil-collab.github.io/blundercheck/

Everything runs in your browser. Games and reviews are stored only on your device.
